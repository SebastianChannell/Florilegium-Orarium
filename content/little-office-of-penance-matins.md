### Psalmus 6 — *Domine, ne in furore*

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Domine, ne in furore tuo arguas me: neque in ira tua corripias me.<br>Miserere mei, Domine, quoniam infirmus sum: sana me, Domine, quoniam conturbata sunt ossa mea.<br>Et anima mea turbata est valde: sed tu, Domine, usquequo?<br>Convertere, Domine, et eripe animam meam: salvum me fac propter misericordiam tuam.<br>Quoniam non est in morte qui memor sit tui: in inferno autem quis confitebitur tibi?<br>Laboravi in gemitu meo; lavabo per singulas noctes lectum meum: lacrimis meis stratum meum rigabo.<br>Turbatus est a furore oculus meus: inveteravi inter omnes inimicos meos.<br>Discedite a me, omnes qui operamini iniquitatem: quoniam exaudivit Dominus vocem fletus mei.<br>Exaudivit Dominus deprecationem meam: Dominus orationem meam suscepit.<br>Erubescant et conturbentur vehementer omnes inimici mei: convertantur et erubescant valde velociter.<br><br>Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto.<br>Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen. | O Lord, rebuke me not in thy indignation: nor chastise me in thy wrath.<br>Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am weak: heal me, O Lord, for my bones are troubled.<br>And my soul is troubled exceedingly: but thou, O Lord, how long?<br>Turn to me, O Lord, and deliver my soul: save me for thy mercy’s sake.<br>For there is no one in death that is mindful of thee: and who shall confess to thee in hell?<br>I have laboured in my groanings; every night I will wash my bed: I will water my couch with my tears.<br>My eye is troubled through indignation: I have grown old among all my enemies.<br>Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity: for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping.<br>The Lord hath heard my supplication: the Lord hath received my prayer.<br>Let all my enemies be ashamed and greatly troubled: let them be turned back and speedily put to shame.<br><br>Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.<br>As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. |

#### Lectio I — Lesson I
| *Ex Oratione Manassæ* | *from the Prayer of Manasses* |
|---|---|
| Domine omnipotens, qui ligasti mare verbo præcepti tui, qui conclusisti abyssum, et signasti eam terribili et laudabili nomine tuo, quem omnia pavent et tremunt a vultu virtutis tuæ; quia importabilis est magnificentia gloriæ tuæ, et insustentabilis ira comminationis tuæ super peccatores: immensa vero et investigabilis misericordia promissionis tuæ. Tu igitur, Domine Deus justorum, non posuisti pœnitentiam justis, sed propter me peccatorem, quoniam peccavi super numerum arenæ maris, et non sum dignus intueri altitudinem cæli. Incurvatus sum vinculo ferreo, ut non possim attollere caput meum, et non est respiratio mihi. Et nunc flecto genua cordis mei, precans a te bonitatem: quia tu es Deus pœnitentium, et in me ostendis omnem misericordiam tuam, quia tu indignum salvabis me, et laudabo te omnibus diebus vitæ meæ; quia te laudat omnis virtus cælorum, et tibi est gloria in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.<br><br>Tu autem, Domine, miserere nobis.<br>**R.** Deo gratias. | O almighty Lord, who hast bound the sea by the word of thy commandment, who hast shut up the deep and sealed it by thy terrible and glorious name, whom all things fear and tremble before the face of thy power; for the majesty of thy glory cannot be borne, and the anger of thy threatening against sinners is insupportable: but the mercy of thy promise is immeasurable and unsearchable. Thou therefore, O Lord, God of the just, hast not appointed repentance for the just, but for me, a sinner; for I have sinned above the number of the sands of the sea, and I am not worthy to behold the height of heaven. I am bowed down with a band of iron, so that I cannot lift up my head, neither is there any breathing for me. And now I bend the knees of my heart, beseeching goodness of thee: for thou art the God of penitents, and in me thou wilt show all thy mercy; for thou wilt save me who am unworthy, and I will praise thee all the days of my life; for all the host of heaven praiseth thee, and unto thee is glory for ever and ever. Amen.<br><br>But thou, O Lord, have mercy upon us.<br>**R.** Thanks be to God. |

### Oratio — Collect

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Deus, cui proprium est misereri semper et parcere, suscipe deprecationem nostram: ut nos et omnes famulos tuos, quos delictorum catena constringit, miseratio tuæ pietatis clementer absolvat. Per Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum Filium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate Spiritus Sancti Deus, per omnia sæcula sæculorum. Amen. | O God, whose property is always to have mercy and to spare, receive our petition: that the compassion of thy loving-kindness may mercifully loose us and all thy servants who are bound by the chain of sin.
