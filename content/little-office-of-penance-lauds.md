### Psalmus 31 — *Beati quorum remissæ sunt*

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Beati quorum remissæ sunt iniquitates: et quorum tecta sunt peccata.<br>Beatus vir cui non imputavit Dominus peccatum: nec est in spiritu ejus dolus.<br>Quoniam tacui, inveteraverunt ossa mea: dum clamarem tota die.<br>Quoniam die ac nocte gravata est super me manus tua: conversus sum in ærumna mea, dum configitur spina.<br>Delictum meum cognitum tibi feci: et injustitiam meam non abscondi.<br>Dixi: Confitebor adversum me injustitiam meam Domino: et tu remisisti impietatem peccati mei.<br>Pro hac orabit ad te omnis sanctus: in tempore opportuno.<br>Verumtamen in diluvio aquarum multarum: ad eum non approximabunt.<br>Tu es refugium meum a tribulatione quæ circumdedit me: exsultatio mea, erue me a circumdantibus me.<br>Intellectum tibi dabo, et instruam te in via hac qua gradieris: firmabo super te oculos meos.<br>Nolite fieri sicut equus et mulus: quibus non est intellectus.<br>In camo et freno maxillas eorum constringe: qui non approximant ad te.<br>Multa flagella peccatoris: sperantem autem in Domino misericordia circumdabit.<br>Lætamini in Domino et exsultate, justi: et gloriamini, omnes recti corde.<br><br>Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto.<br>Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Tu es refugium * meum, Domine, a tribulatione quæ circumdedit me. | Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven: and whose sins are covered.<br>Blessed is the man to whom the Lord hath not imputed sin: and in whose spirit there is no guile.<br>Because I was silent, my bones grew old: whilst I cried out all the day long.<br>For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me: I was turned in my anguish, whilst the thorn was fastened.<br>I have acknowledged my sin to thee: and my injustice I have not concealed.<br>I said: I will confess against myself my injustice to the Lord: and thou hast forgiven the wickedness of my sin.<br>For this shall every one that is holy pray to thee: in a seasonable time.<br>But in a flood of many waters: they shall not come near unto him.<br>Thou art my refuge from the trouble which hath encompassed me: my joy, deliver me from them that surround me.<br>I will give thee understanding, and I will instruct thee in this way in which thou shalt go: I will fix my eyes upon thee.<br>Do not become like the horse and the mule: who have no understanding.<br>With bit and bridle bind fast their jaws: who come not near unto thee.<br>Many are the scourges of the sinner: but mercy shall encompass him that hopeth in the Lord.<br>Be glad in the Lord and rejoice, ye just: and glory, all ye right of heart.<br><br>Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.<br>As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Thou art my refuge, O Lord, from the tribulation which hath surrounded me. |

### Canticum Zachariæ — *Benedictus*

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Benedictus Dominus Deus Israel: quia visitavit et fecit redemptionem plebis suæ.<br>Et erexit cornu salutis nobis: in domo David pueri sui.<br>Sicut locutus est per os sanctorum, qui a sæculo sunt, prophetarum ejus.<br>Salutem ex inimicis nostris: et de manu omnium qui oderunt nos.<br>Ad faciendam misericordiam cum patribus nostris: et memorari testamenti sui sancti.<br>Jusjurandum quod juravit ad Abraham patrem nostrum: daturum se nobis.<br>Ut sine timore, de manu inimicorum nostrorum liberati: serviamus illi.<br>In sanctitate et justitia coram ipso: omnibus diebus nostris.<br>Et tu, puer, Propheta Altissimi vocaberis: præibis enim ante faciem Domini parare vias ejus.<br>Ad dandam scientiam salutis plebi ejus: in remissionem peccatorum eorum.<br>Per viscera misericordiæ Dei nostri: in quibus visitavit nos Oriens ex alto.<br>Illuminare his qui in tenebris et in umbra mortis sedent: ad dirigendos pedes nostros in viam pacis.<br><br>Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto.<br>Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Vivo ego, * dicit Dominus: nolo mortem peccatoris, sed magis ut convertatur et vivat. | Blessed be the Lord God of Israel: because he hath visited and wrought the redemption of his people.<br>And hath raised up a horn of salvation to us: in the house of David his servant.<br>As he spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets, who are from the beginning.<br>Salvation from our enemies: and from the hand of all that hate us.<br>To perform mercy to our fathers: and to remember his holy testament.<br>The oath which he swore to Abraham our father: that he would grant to us.<br>That being delivered from the hand of our enemies, we may serve him without fear.<br>In holiness and justice before him: all our days.<br>And thou, child, shalt be called the prophet of the Highest: for thou shalt go before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways.<br>To give knowledge of salvation to his people: unto the remission of their sins.<br>Through the bowels of the mercy of our God: in which the Orient from on high hath visited us.<br>To enlighten them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death: to direct our feet into the way of peace.<br><br>Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.<br>As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** As I live, saith the Lord: I desire not the death of the sinner, but rather that he be converted and live. |

### Oratio — Collect

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Exaudi, quæsumus, Domine, supplicum preces, et confitentium tibi parce peccatis: ut pariter nobis indulgentiam tribuas benignus, et pacem. Per Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum Filium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate Spiritus Sancti Deus, per omnia sæcula sæculorum. Amen. | Hear, we beseech thee, O Lord, the prayers of thy suppliants, and pardon the sins of those who confess to thee: that in thy goodness thou mayest grant us both forgiveness and peace. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen. |
