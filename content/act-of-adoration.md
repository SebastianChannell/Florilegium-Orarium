O great God, the sovereign Lord of heaven and earth, I prostrate myself before Thee. With all the angels and Saints, I acknowledge Thee to be my Creator and sovereign Lord, my first beginning and last end. I render to Thee the homage of my life. I submit myself to Thy holy Will, and I devote myself to Thy divine service this day and for ever.
