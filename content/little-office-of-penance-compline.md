### Psalmus 87 — *Domine Deus salutis meæ*

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Domine Deus salutis meæ: in die clamavi et nocte coram te.<br>Intret in conspectu tuo oratio mea: inclina aurem tuam ad precem meam.<br>Quia repleta est malis anima mea: et vita mea inferno appropinquavit.<br>Æstimatus sum cum descendentibus in lacum: factus sum sicut homo sine adjutorio, inter mortuos liber.<br>Sicut vulnerati dormientes in sepulcris, quorum non es memor amplius: et ipsi de manu tua repulsi sunt.<br>Posuerunt me in lacu inferiori: in tenebrosis et in umbra mortis.<br>Super me confirmatus est furor tuus: et omnes fluctus tuos induxisti super me.<br>Longe fecisti notos meos a me: posuerunt me abominationem sibi.<br>Traditus sum, et non egrediebar: oculi mei languerunt præ inopia.<br>Clamavi ad te, Domine, tota die: expandi ad te manus meas.<br>Numquid mortuis facies mirabilia: aut medici suscitabunt et confitebuntur tibi?<br>Numquid narrabit aliquis in sepulcro misericordiam tuam: et veritatem tuam in perditione?<br>Numquid cognoscentur in tenebris mirabilia tua: et justitia tua in terra oblivionis?<br>Et ego ad te, Domine, clamavi: et mane oratio mea præveniet te.<br>Ut quid, Domine, repellis orationem meam: avertis faciem tuam a me?<br>Pauper sum ego et in laboribus a juventute mea: exaltatus autem, humiliatus sum et conturbatus.<br>In me transierunt iræ tuæ: et terrores tui conturbaverunt me.<br>Circumdederunt me sicut aqua tota die: circumdederunt me simul.<br>Elongasti a me amicum et proximum: et notos meos a miseria.<br><br>Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto.<br>Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Iniquitatem meam ego cognosco, * et peccatum meum contra me est semper. | O Lord, the God of my salvation: I have cried in the day and in the night before thee.<br>Let my prayer come in before thee: incline thine ear to my petition.<br>For my soul is filled with evils: and my life hath drawn nigh to hell.<br>I am counted among them that go down to the pit: I am become as a man without help, free among the dead.<br>Like the slain sleeping in the sepulchres, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cast off from thy hand.<br>They have laid me in the lower pit: in the dark places and in the shadow of death.<br>Thy wrath is strong over me: and all thy waves thou hast brought in upon me.<br>Thou hast put away my acquaintance far from me: they have set me an abomination to themselves.<br>I was delivered up and came not forth: my eyes languished through poverty.<br>All the day I cried to thee, O Lord: I stretched out my hands to thee.<br>Wilt thou show wonders to the dead: or shall physicians raise them to life, and they praise thee?<br>Shall any one in the sepulchre declare thy mercy: and thy truth in destruction?<br>Shall thy wonders be known in the dark: and thy justice in the land of forgetfulness?<br>But I, O Lord, have cried to thee: and in the morning my prayer shall come before thee.<br>Lord, why castest thou off my prayer: why turnest thou away thy face from me?<br>I am poor and in labours from my youth: and being exalted, I have been humbled and troubled.<br>Thy wrath hath come upon me: and thy terrors have troubled me.<br>They have come round about me like water all the day: they have compassed me about together.<br>Friend and neighbour thou hast put far from me: and my acquaintance because of misery.<br><br>Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.<br>As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** I know my iniquity, and my sin is always before me. |

### Canticum Simeonis — *Nunc dimittis*

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Nunc dimittis servum tuum, Domine: secundum verbum tuum in pace.<br>Quia viderunt oculi mei: salutare tuum.<br>Quod parasti: ante faciem omnium populorum.<br>Lumen ad revelationem gentium: et gloriam plebis tuæ Israel.<br><br>Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto.<br>Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Salva nos, * Domine, vigilantes; custodi nos dormientes: ut vigilemus cum Christo et requiescamus in pace. | Now thou dost dismiss thy servant, O Lord: according to thy word in peace.<br>Because my eyes have seen: thy salvation.<br>Which thou hast prepared: before the face of all peoples.<br>A light to the revelation of the Gentiles: and the glory of thy people Israel.<br><br>Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.<br>As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Save us, O Lord, while we are awake; guard us while we sleep: that we may watch with Christ and rest in peace. |

### Oratio — Prayer

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Domine Jesu Christe, qui salvas omnes et neminem vis perire, et cui nunquam sine spe misericordiæ supplicatur; dixisti enim ore tuo sancto et benedicto: Quidquid petieritis in nomine meo, fiet vobis. Precor te propter nomen sanctum tuum, ut in articulo mortis meæ des mihi integritatem sensus cum loquela, vehementem cordis contritionem de peccatis meis, veram fidem, spem ordinatam, caritatem perfectam: ut tibi puro corde dicere valeam: In manus tuas, Domine, commendo spiritum meum; qui es benedictus et gloriosus in sæcula sæculorum. Amen. | O Lord Jesus Christ, who savest all and willest that none should perish, and to whom none ever prayeth without hope of mercy; for thou hast said with thy holy and blessed mouth: Whatsoever you shall ask in my name shall be done unto you. I beseech thee for the sake of thy holy name, that at the hour of my death thou wouldst grant me the sound use of my senses and speech, a vehement contrition of heart for my sins, true faith, rightly ordered hope, and perfect charity: that with a pure heart I may be able to say unto thee: Into thy hands, O Lord, I commend my spirit; who art blessed and glorious for ever and ever. Amen. |

### Alia Oratio — Another Prayer

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Visita, quæsumus, Domine, habitationem istam, et omnes insidias inimici ab ea longe repelle: Angeli tui sancti habitent in ea, qui nos in pace custodiant; et benedictio tua sit super nos semper. Per Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum Filium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate Spiritus Sancti Deus, per omnia sæcula sæculorum. Amen. | Visit, we beseech thee, O Lord, this dwelling, and drive far from it all the snares of the enemy: let thy holy Angels dwell herein, to preserve us in peace; and let thy blessing be always upon us. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen. |
