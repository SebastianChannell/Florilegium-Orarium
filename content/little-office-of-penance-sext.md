### Psalmus 101 — *Domine, exaudi orationem meam*

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Domine, exaudi orationem meam: et clamor meus ad te veniat.<br>Non avertas faciem tuam a me: in quacumque die tribulor, inclina ad me aurem tuam.<br>In quacumque die invocavero te: velociter exaudi me.<br>Quia defecerunt sicut fumus dies mei: et ossa mea sicut cremium aruerunt.<br>Percussus sum ut fœnum, et aruit cor meum: quia oblitus sum comedere panem meum.<br>A voce gemitus mei: adhæsit os meum carni meæ.<br>Similis factus sum pellicano solitudinis: factus sum sicut nycticorax in domicilio.<br>Vigilavi: et factus sum sicut passer solitarius in tecto.<br>Tota die exprobrabant mihi inimici mei: et qui laudabant me adversum me jurabant.<br>Quia cinerem tamquam panem manducabam: et potum meum cum fletu miscebam.<br>A facie iræ et indignationis tuæ: quia elevans allisisti me.<br>Dies mei sicut umbra declinaverunt: et ego sicut fœnum arui.<br>Tu autem, Domine, in æternum permanes: et memoriale tuum in generationem et generationem.<br>Tu exsurgens misereberis Sion: quia tempus miserendi ejus, quia venit tempus.<br>Quoniam placuerunt servis tuis lapides ejus: et terræ ejus miserebuntur.<br>Et timebunt gentes nomen tuum, Domine: et omnes reges terræ gloriam tuam.<br>Quia ædificavit Dominus Sion: et videbitur in gloria sua.<br>Respexit in orationem humilium: et non sprevit precem eorum.<br>Scribantur hæc in generatione altera: et populus qui creabitur laudabit Dominum.<br>Quia prospexit de excelso sancto suo: Dominus de cælo in terram aspexit.<br>Ut audiret gemitus compeditorum: ut solveret filios interemptorum.<br>Ut annuntient in Sion nomen Domini: et laudem ejus in Jerusalem.<br>In conveniendo populos in unum: et reges ut serviant Domino.<br>Respondit ei in via virtutis suæ: Paucitatem dierum meorum nuntia mihi.<br>Ne revoces me in dimidio dierum meorum: in generationem et generationem anni tui.<br>Initio tu, Domine, terram fundasti: et opera manuum tuarum sunt cæli.<br>Ipsi peribunt, tu autem permanes: et omnes sicut vestimentum veterascent.<br>Et sicut opertorium mutabis eos, et mutabuntur: tu autem idem ipse es, et anni tui non deficient.<br>Filii servorum tuorum habitabunt: et semen eorum in sæculum dirigetur.<br><br>Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto.<br>Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Averte faciem * tuam a peccatis meis, Domine, et omnes iniquitates meas dele. | O Lord, hear my prayer: and let my cry come to thee.<br>Turn not away thy face from me: in the day when I am in trouble, incline thine ear to me.<br>In what day soever I shall call upon thee: hear me speedily.<br>For my days are vanished like smoke: and my bones are grown dry like fuel for the fire.<br>I am smitten as grass, and my heart is withered: because I forgot to eat my bread.<br>Through the voice of my groaning: my bone hath cleaved to my flesh.<br>I am become like to a pelican of the wilderness: I am like a night raven in the house.<br>I have watched: and am become as a sparrow all alone on the housetop.<br>All the day long my enemies reproached me: and they that praised me swore against me.<br>For I ate ashes like bread: and mingled my drink with weeping.<br>Because of thy anger and indignation: for having lifted me up thou hast thrown me down.<br>My days have declined like a shadow: and I am withered like grass.<br>But thou, O Lord, endurest for ever: and thy memorial from generation to generation.<br>Thou shalt arise and have mercy on Sion: for it is time to have mercy on it, for the time is come.<br>For the stones thereof have pleased thy servants: and they shall have pity on the earth thereof.<br>And the Gentiles shall fear thy name, O Lord: and all the kings of the earth thy glory.<br>For the Lord hath built up Sion: and he shall be seen in his glory.<br>He hath had regard to the prayer of the humble: and he hath not despised their petition.<br>Let these things be written unto another generation: and the people that shall be created shall praise the Lord.<br>Because he hath looked forth from his high sanctuary: from heaven the Lord hath looked upon the earth.<br>That he might hear the groans of them that are in fetters: that he might release the children of the slain.<br>That they may declare the name of the Lord in Sion: and his praise in Jerusalem.<br>When the people assemble together: and kings, to serve the Lord.<br>He answered him in the way of his strength: Declare unto me the fewness of my days.<br>Call me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are unto generation and generation.<br>In the beginning, O Lord, thou foundedst the earth: and the heavens are the works of thy hands.<br>They shall perish, but thou remainest: and all of them shall grow old like a garment.<br>And as a vesture thou shalt change them, and they shall be changed: but thou art always the selfsame, and thy years shall not fail.<br>The children of thy servants shall continue: and their seed shall be directed for ever.<br><br>Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.<br>As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. ### Capitulum — Jeremias 2 — Little Chapter

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Arguet te malitia tua, et aversio tua increpabit te: scito et vide quia malum et amarum est reliquisse te Dominum Deum tuum, et non esse timorem mei apud te, dicit Dominus Deus exercituum.<br><br>**R.** Deo gratias.<br><br>**V.** Domine, non secundum peccata nostra facias nobis.<br>**R.** Neque secundum iniquitates nostras retribuas nobis. | Thy own wickedness shall reprove thee, and thy apostasy shall rebuke thee: know thou and see that it is an evil and a bitter thing for thee to have left the Lord thy God, and that my fear is not with thee, saith the Lord, the God of hosts.<br><br>**R.** Thanks be to God.<br><br>**V.** O Lord, deal not with us according to our sins.<br>**R.** Neither reward us according to our iniquities. |

### Oratio — Collect

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Absolve, quæsumus, Domine, nostrorum vincula peccatorum: et quidquid pro eis meremur averte. Per Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum Filium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate Spiritus Sancti Deus, per omnia sæcula sæculorum. Amen. | Loose, we beseech thee, O Lord, the bonds of our sins: and turn away whatsoever we deserve for them. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen. |
