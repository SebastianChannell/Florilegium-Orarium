title: Ambrosian Prayer to the Holy Ghost
type: prayer
devotion: Holy Ghost
search: Nunc Sancte nobis Spiritus, Saint Ambrose, Holy Spirit, Paraclete
---
Now, O Holy Spirit, who art one
With the Father and the Son,
Deign to be infused into us without delay
Poured once more into our souls.
Let lips, tongue, soul, senses and strength
Sing out their acknowledgment of God.
May our love be set aflame with the fire of your Love,
May its heat inflame our neighbors.
Through you grant that we may know
The Father and acknowledge the Son
Grant, too, that we may always believe in you,
The Spirit who proceeds from both.
Amen.
