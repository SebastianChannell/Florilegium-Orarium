## LA

Parce, Dómine, parce pópulo tuo:
ne in ætérnum irascáris nobis.

Flectámus iram víndicem,
plorémus ante Júdicem;
clamémus ore súpplici,
dicámus omnes cérnui:

Parce, Dómine, parce pópulo tuo:
ne in ætérnum irascáris nobis.

Nostris malis offéndimus
tuam, Deus, cleméntiam;
effúnde nobis désuper,
remíssor, indulgéntiam.

Parce, Dómine, parce pópulo tuo:
ne in ætérnum irascáris nobis.

Dans tempus acceptábile,
da lacrimárum rívulis
laváre cordis víctimam,
quam læta adúrat cáritas.

Parce, Dómine, parce pópulo tuo:
ne in ætérnum irascáris nobis.

Audi, benígne Cónditor,
nostras preces cum flétibus,
in hoc sacro jejúnio
fusas quadragenário.

Parce, Dómine, parce pópulo tuo:
ne in ætérnum irascáris nobis.

## EN

Spare, O Lord, spare Thy people:
be not angry with us for ever.

Let us bend the avenging wrath,
let us weep before the Judge;
let us cry with suppliant voice,
let us all, bowed down, say:

Spare, O Lord, spare Thy people:
be not angry with us for ever.

By our evils we have offended
Thy clemency, O God;
pour down upon us from on high,
O Forgiver, Thy pardon.

Spare, O Lord, spare Thy people:
be not angry with us for ever.

Granting a time acceptable,
grant that by streams of tears
we may wash the sacrifice of the heart,
which joyful charity burns up.

Spare, O Lord, spare Thy people:
be not angry with us for ever.

Hear, O gracious Creator,
our prayers with weeping,
poured forth in this sacred
forty-day fast.

Spare, O Lord, spare Thy people:
be not angry with us for ever.
