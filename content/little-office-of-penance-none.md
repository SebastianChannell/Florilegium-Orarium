### Psalmus 129 — *De profundis*

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| De profundis clamavi ad te, Domine: Domine, exaudi vocem meam.<br>Fiant aures tuæ intendentes: in vocem deprecationis meæ.<br>Si iniquitates observaveris, Domine: Domine, quis sustinebit?<br>Quia apud te propitiatio est: et propter legem tuam sustinui te, Domine.<br>Sustinuit anima mea in verbo ejus: speravit anima mea in Domino.<br>A custodia matutina usque ad noctem: speret Israel in Domino.<br>Quia apud Dominum misericordia: et copiosa apud eum redemptio.<br>Et ipse redimet Israel: ex omnibus iniquitatibus ejus.<br><br>Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto.<br>Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** Si iniquitates * observaveris, Domine: Domine, quis sustinebit? | Out of the depths I have cried to thee, O Lord: Lord, hear my voice.<br>Let thine ears be attentive: to the voice of my supplication.<br>If thou, O Lord, wilt mark iniquities: Lord, who shall endure it?<br>For with thee there is merciful forgiveness: and by reason of thy law I have waited for thee, O Lord.<br>My soul hath relied on his word: my soul hath hoped in the Lord.<br>From the morning watch even until night: let Israel hope in the Lord.<br>Because with the Lord there is mercy: and with him plentiful redemption.<br>And he shall redeem Israel: from all his iniquities.<br><br>Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.<br>As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.<br><br>**Ant.** If thou, O Lord, wilt mark iniquities: Lord, who shall endure it? |

### Capitulum — Tobias 3 — Little Chapter

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Ne reminiscaris, Domine, delicta nostra vel parentum nostrorum: neque vindictam sumas de peccatis nostris.<br><br>**R.** Deo gratias.<br><br>**V.** Ab occultis meis munda me, Domine.<br>**R.** Et ab alienis parce servo tuo. | Remember not, O Lord, our offences, nor those of our parents: neither take vengeance on our sins.<br><br>**R.** Thanks be to God.<br><br>**V.** From my secret sins cleanse me, O Lord.<br>**R.** And from those of others spare thy servant. |

### Oratio — Collect

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Propitiare, Domine, supplicationibus nostris, et animarum nostrarum medere languoribus: ut, remissione percepta, in tua semper benedictione lætemur. Per Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum Filium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate Spiritus Sancti Deus, per omnia sæcula sæculorum. Amen. | Be favourable, O Lord, to our supplications, and heal the infirmities of our souls: that, having received forgiveness, we may ever rejoice in thy blessing. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen. |
