## Commendatio — The Commendation

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Supplices offerimus<br>Tibi, Virgo pia,<br>Hæc laudum præconia:<br>Fac nos ut in via, | These praises and prayers<br>I lay at thy feet,<br>O Virgin of virgins!<br>O Mary most sweet! |
| Ducas cursu prospero;<br>Et in agonia<br>Tu nobis assiste,<br>O dulcis Maria. | Be thou my true guide<br>Through this pilgrimage here;<br>And stand by my side<br>When death draweth near. |

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| **R.** Deo gratias. | **R.** Thanks be to God. |

| **V.** In conceptione tua, Virgo, immaculata fuisti. | **V.** In thy conception, O Virgin! thou wast immaculate. |
| **R.** Ora pro nobis Patrem, cujus Filium peperisti. | **R.** Pray for us to the Father, whose Son thou didst bring forth. |

## Oremus — Let Us Pray

| Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Deus, qui per immaculatam Virginis conceptionem dignum Filio tuo habitaculum præparasti: quæsumus, ut qui ex morte ejusdem Filii tui prævisa eam ab omni labe præservasti, nos quoque mundos ejus intercessione ad te pervenire concedas. Per eumdem Christum Dominum nostrum. | O God Who, by the immaculate Conception of the Virgin, didst prepare a worthy habitation for Thy Son: we beseech Thee, that, as in view of the death of that Son, Thou didst preserve her from all stain of sin, so Thou wouldst enable us, being made pure by her intercession, to come unto Thee. Through the same Christ our Lord. |
| **R.** Amen. | **R.** Amen. |
